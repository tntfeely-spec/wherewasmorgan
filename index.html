Morgan Bauer left Aberdeen, South Dakota for Georgia on February 12, 2016.
She was 19.
She disappeared on February 26.
Both people charged in her case have been convicted.

Where the case stands

W
Two people were charged in Morgan’s death.
One pleaded guilty before trial and is serving life without parole.
The other was tried in Newton County, Georgia in August 2026. On August 31, the jury returned guilty verdicts on all five counts.
Life without parole, handed down on September 1, 2026.
Both are serving their sentences.

The podcast

The search for Morgan happened in Facebook Messenger: 6,553 messages across dozens of conversations. The podcast follows it as it happened.
Three episodes are out now.
W
“Wish you could see how beautiful it is here” Morgan’s story: Aberdeen, her best friend Taryn, the move to Georgia, and the couple she found on Craigslist. “Why would you think for all these years she would be doing her own thing?” A Facebook conversation, a story that had been rehearsed, and the long assumption that Morgan had run away. “Sasha has been calling me” Morgan’s stage name, a Facebook account called Sasha Akins, and the question of who was behind it.
