Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Where Was Morgan? is a podcast and information site about Morgan Bauer. It is run by Colbridges Entertainment, LLC (“we” or “us”). This policy explains what information we handle when you visit wherewasmorgan.com (the “site”).

Information we collect

Information you send us. If you email contact@wherewasmorgan.com, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write. We use it to read and answer your message.

Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider, Hostinger, keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, the pages requested, and the date and time. These logs are used to run and secure the site. We do not use them to identify individual visitors.

The site has no accounts, sign-ups, or comment forms.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools. Our hosting provider may use technical cookies or similar technology that are needed to deliver and protect the site. If we add analytics in the future, we will update this policy first.

How we use information

  • To read and respond to messages.
  • To run, secure, and maintain the site.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run the site and our email, which are Hostinger and its email service, and when the law requires it or to protect people’s safety and rights.

The site links to other websites and apps, including Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Court TV, and YouTube. Once you leave our site, those services’ own privacy policies apply.

Messages are not confidential

Email is not a secure channel, and we cannot promise that messages stay private. Please do not send financial details, government ID numbers, or health information. We are not law enforcement. If you have information about a crime, contact your local police or the authorities.

How long we keep information

We keep emails only as long as we need them to respond or to keep a reasonable record, and then delete them. Server logs are kept by our hosting provider for the period set in its own policies.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted. Email us at contact@wherewasmorgan.com and we will respond. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of transmitting or storing data online is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.
